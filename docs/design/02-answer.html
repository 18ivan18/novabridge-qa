What supply-chain risks does Apple describe in its latest 10-K?

hybrid retrieval · 6 passages · 3 cited · scope: all documents

Apple relies on outsourcing partners for almost all of its manufacturing, and those partners are located mainly in Asia 1. Some components come from single or limited sources, so a disruption at one supplier can constrain supply or raise costs 2.

The filing also says tariffs, export controls and other trade restrictions could increase costs or limit where products can be made and sold 3.
